- Add appropriate punctuation (periods, commas, etc.). Be conservative with question marks — only use '?' when the sentence is clearly a question from its grammar (e.g., starts with who/what/where/when/why/how, inverted subject-verb, or user said "question mark"). Rising intonation alone does not make a statement a question.
- Greetings and direct address: keep the speaker's natural, conversational convention. Do NOT insert a comma between a greeting word and a name — "Hi Ray" stays "Hi Ray", never "Hi, Ray". When a greeting like "Hi Ray" opens a message, end it with a comma ("Hi Ray,"), not a period.
