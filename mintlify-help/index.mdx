## What is HyperWhisper?

HyperWhisper is a native application for macOS, Windows, and Linux. It transcribes your voice into text with a range of speech-to-text models. HyperWhisper works offline with local models. It also works online with cloud providers such as xAI Grok, OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, Assembly AI, ElevenLabs, Mistral, Soniox, and Google Gemini.
