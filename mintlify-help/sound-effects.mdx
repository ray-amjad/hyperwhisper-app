---
title: Sound Effects
description: Audio feedback that confirms when HyperWhisper starts and stops recording.
---

HyperWhisper plays a short chime when you start a recording. It plays a different chime when you stop the recording. These sounds tell you when the app records your voice. You do not have to look at the screen.

## Turn sound effects on or off

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="macOS">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings">
        Click the HyperWhisper menu bar icon, then open **Settings**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Go to Sound">
        Select the **Sound** section.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Toggle Sound Effects">
        Turn **Sound Effects** on or off. If you turn it off, the app plays no sounds during a recording.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Windows">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings">
        Right-click the HyperWhisper tray icon, then choose **Settings**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Go to Sound">
        Select **Sound** from the sidebar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Toggle Sound Effects">
        Check or uncheck **Sound Effects**. If the box is not checked, the app plays no sounds during a recording.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Linux">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings">
        Open the main window, then click **Settings**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Go to Audio and desktop">
        Scroll to the **Audio and desktop** section.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Toggle the sounds">
        Check or uncheck **Play recording start and stop sounds**. The box is checked by default. Then click **Save settings**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## The two chimes

The app plays two different chimes:

- **Start recording** — The app plays a chime when you press your recording hotkey and the app starts to record.
- **Stop recording** — The app plays a different chime when you release the hotkey or click stop.

The app loads the sounds at startup. As a result, each chime plays immediately, with no delay that you can hear.

## Sound themes (macOS only)

The app has two sound themes:

| Theme | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Classic** | The traditional system sounds |
| **New** | Modern, quiet sounds |

<Note>
  The app stores a theme selection internally, but playback does not read it yet — every chime uses the same fixed sound file regardless of the stored theme. The Settings window also does not show a theme control. A future release will wire the theme setting up and add this control.
</Note>

## Sound effects volume

The volume of the sound effects is separate from the system volume. The scale is 0.0 to 1.0.

| Platform | Control | Default |
|---|---|---|
| macOS | No slider in Settings yet. The stored value is fixed at the default | 0.5 (half volume) |
| Windows | No control. The volume is fixed | 0.5 (half volume) |
| Linux | A **Sound effect volume** slider in **Settings → Audio and desktop** | 1.0 (full volume) |

On Linux, drag the slider between 0.0 and 1.0, then click **Save settings**. The slider is disabled while **Play recording start and stop sounds** is unchecked, because a volume has no effect when the sounds are off.

<Note>
  A future macOS release will add the slider to the Settings window. Until then, the macOS volume stays at 0.5.
</Note>
